About SAAGAR

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · sagardahal123.com.np

SAAGAR is a free website made in Nepal, for Nepal. It puts four everyday needs in one place: games you can play alone, against a bot, or online with a friend; tools that finish small but annoying jobs (date conversion, tax, unit conversion, PDF and photo fixes); skills guides that show a beginner exactly where to start; and Nepal law explainers written in plain language, with the act and dafa number so you can check the original yourself.

Hi, I am the SAAGAR mascot.

I wave when you arrive, cheer when you win, think when a page is empty, and sulk when something breaks. If you see me sulking, please tell us through the report page.

Why this site exists

About half of Nepal is online, and most people reach the internet on a cheap Android phone with a limited data pack. Big foreign websites assume fast data and expensive devices. SAAGAR is built the other way round: small pages, no sign-up, no app install needed, everything that can run inside your browser does. A monthly data pack should be enough to enjoy the whole site.

What is on the site today

  • 13 games — Ludo, Carrom, Teen Patti (play chips only), Snakes & Ladders, Checkers, Connect 4, Tic-Tac-Toe, Neon Snake, 2048, Rock-Paper-Scissors, Guess the Number, Memory Match and Reaction Duel. Every game works solo or against a bot, and most can be played in an online room with a shareable link.
  • 25 tools — Bikram Sambat ⇄ AD dates, age, percentage, GPA, land and gold units, foreign exchange and gold rates, salary tax, EMI and fixed deposits, QR codes, PDF, photo, compress, scan, OCR, signature, Preeti ⇄ Unicode, word tools and more.
  • Skills — short roadmaps for real skills, with what to learn first and where to learn it for free.
  • Nepal laws — situations explained with the law that applies, what to do and who to contact. General information, not legal advice.

How it stays free

SAAGAR shows advertising served by Google AdSense. That is the whole business model — no accounts, no paid plans, no data selling. Ads do not change game results or tool calculations.

Play money only

Some games look like classic Nepali card and board games. They use play chips only. There is no cash, no wallet, no deposit, no withdrawal, and no prize of money value anywhere on this site. Chips and badges are cosmetic and reset-able.

Who runs it

SAAGAR is built and maintained by Sagar Dahal in Nepal. It is a small project: one person, a plain text editor, and a lot of testing on a real phone at 360 pixels wide.

Talk to us

Bugs, wrong numbers, unclear Nepali wording, a law that has changed, or a feature you want — all of it is welcome. Use the contact page or the report page. Real messages get real replies.
