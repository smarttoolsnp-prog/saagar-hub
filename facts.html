🌌 Space

  • Neutron stars are so dense that one teaspoon would weigh about a billion tonnes.
  • There are more trees on Earth (~3 trillion) than stars in the Milky Way (~100–400 billion).
  • One day on Venus is longer than its year — it rotates slower than it orbits the Sun.
  • Space is completely silent — there's no air for sound to travel through.
  • If you could fold a piece of paper 42 times, it would reach the Moon.

🧬 Human body

  • Your body produces about 25 million new cells every second.
  • Information travels along your nerves at up to 430 km/h.
  • Your stomach gets a new lining every few days — otherwise it would digest itself.
  • You blink about 20 times per minute — that's 10 million+ blinks a year.

🕰️ History & world

  • Sharks are older than trees — they existed before trees existed.
  • Honey never spoils — 3,000-year-old honey found in Egyptian tombs was still edible.
  • Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood.
  • Bananas are berries, but strawberries are not.
  • The Eiffel Tower grows about 15 cm taller in summer due to heat expansion.
