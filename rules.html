Content & Community Rules

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · applies to every game room, chat and voice chat

Game rooms are shared spaces. These rules keep them friendly for everyone, including school students playing with friends or a teacher. They apply to room names, chat messages, quick reactions, voice chat, and anything you send us by email.

The short version

Be a decent human. Play fair. Keep it play-money. Do not make the room worse for the next person.

1. Always allowed

  • Playing any game, any mode: solo, vs bot, or a room with friends.
  • Inviting friends with the room link, sharing your result, trash-talking about the game.
  • Speak Nepali, English, or Roman Nepali — all are welcome, in text and voice.
  • Reporting a bug, a wrong number, or a bad actor. Reports are private.

2. Never allowed

  • Hate or harassment: slurs, casteism, sexism, threats, bullying, or targeting anyone for who they are.
  • Sexual content, nudity, or anything involving minors in a sexual way. This is reported without exception.
  • Violence and self-harm: threats of violence, promoting suicide or self-harm, or encouraging dangerous acts.
  • Real-money gambling: SAAGAR runs on play chips only. Offering to bet, buy, sell, top-up or cash out chips is banned, as is promoting any gambling, betting or crypto scheme in a room.
  • Spam and scams: repeated messages, flooding, chain messages, "free data/money" links, job scams, or any link or phone number in chat.
  • Images, files and links in chat: sharing is switched off in chat by design, because it cannot be moderated reliably. Do not try to work around it (for example by spelling out a link).
  • Other people's private information: phone numbers, addresses, school names, photos or ID numbers of anyone else.
  • Impersonation: pretending to be SAAGAR, a moderator, or another player.
  • Cheating or attacking the site: scripting, bots in multiplayer, exploiting bugs for ranked scores, or trying to break, overload or probe the service.

3. Age guidance

  • Games are family-friendly and fine for younger children, ideally with a parent nearby for the online modes.
  • Online rooms are intended for ages 13+. If you tell us you are under 16, chat is limited to preset quick reactions and voice chat is off by default.
  • Voice rooms are for 16+ by default. We do not record audio; rooms are live only.
  • If you are a parent or teacher and want chat or voice disabled for a group, email us — feature switches can be turned off per room or globally.

4. How rooms are kept safe

  • Host controls: the person who creates the room can mute, kick, or lock it.
  • Personal controls: any player can mute or block another player locally.
  • Automatic filters: messages pass through a bad-word filter covering Nepali, Roman Nepali and English, and are rate-limited so one person cannot flood the room.
  • Reports: every room has a report button. Reports go to a private queue that a human reads.
  • Adults-and-children mix: if a room is being used by a school or an event, tell us and we can require quick-reaction-only chat for that room.

5. Enforcement

  1. Warning and mute — for a first, minor slip (for example flooding).
  2. Kick and temporary block — for repeat or serious breaches.
  3. Device-level ban — for threats, sexual content, scams or attacks on the service.
  4. Report to authorities — child safety, credible threats and illegal content are reported, and records are preserved for that purpose.

You can appeal any action once, by email, explaining what happened. We read appeals and correct mistakes.

6. Report something

Use the report page (fastest) or email dahalsaagar8@gmail.com. Include the room code, the time (Nepal time if you can), and what happened. Reports about people are handled before everything else, and you do not have to identify yourself.
