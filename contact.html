Where to send what

  • Bug or something broken — use the report page (it asks for the exact details we need: page, phone, browser, what you tapped).
  • Wrong information (a tax rate, a date, a law, a unit conversion) — email with the page link and, if you have it, the official source that shows the correct figure.
  • Privacy questions — see the privacy policy, then email us; we answer privacy questions in writing.
  • Abuse, threats or bad behaviour in a room or chat — use report and tick "abuse". Reports about people are handled first.
  • Advertising, sponsorship, press, collaboration — email with a subject line starting with "Business".

Other places you will find us

  • Facebook: facebook.com/saagar.dahal.50 — fastest for a quick question or to send a screenshot.
  • Email: dahalsaagar8@gmail.com — best for anything that needs a written record.

How long replies take

Usually within 2 working days. Reports about abuse or a broken game are looked at first. This is a small independent project in Nepal Standard Time (UTC+5:45) — please allow for the time difference if you write from abroad.

If you are writing about a legal page

The Nepal laws section is general information to help you understand a situation — it is not legal advice and it does not create a lawyer-client relationship. For your own case, talk to a licensed advocate or the office named on that page.

Building something with SAAGAR, or you run a school or college and want a game or tool added? Say hello — those messages get replied to the same day when possible.
