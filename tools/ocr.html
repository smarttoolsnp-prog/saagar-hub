💡 Getting a good result

  • Fill the frame with the text and keep the camera square to the page.
  • Good, even light beats a high-res photo — shadows break OCR.
  • Printed text works far better than handwriting.
  • The engine itself (a few MB) downloads the first time you press Extract, so give it a moment on slow connections.
