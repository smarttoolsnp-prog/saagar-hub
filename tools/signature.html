Signature Maker

🖊️ Draw your signature

📄 Tips for an accepted signature

  • Sign in the top part of the box — the dashed line shows where it sits above the name.
  • Use transparent background when you'll place it on a form; use white if the form will be printed.
  • Keep one signature style and reuse it, the way you'd use a pen.
  • Export at a large size — it stays sharp when printed smaller.
