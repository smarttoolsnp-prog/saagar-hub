💡 What to pick

For passport and form photos, 600–800 px wide at 75% is plenty. For a website image, 1200 px WebP looks sharp and stays small. Set a target size and the tool automatically lowers quality until the file fits — try under 100 KB if a portal keeps rejecting your upload.
