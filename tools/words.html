🇳🇵 Why the groups look odd

In Nepal and India, digits group after the first three: 12,34,567 reads as twelve lakh thirty-four thousand five hundred sixty-seven. That's why the words skip from thousand straight to lakh, then crore — there's no “million” in this system.
