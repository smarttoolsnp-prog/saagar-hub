Nepali Text Converter

The classic Preeti, Kantipur and PCS Nepali legacy fonts are supported — conversion runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded. Based on the open-source Shuvayatra/preeti mapping tables.

Preeti is a legacy glyph font, so some rare conjuncts and ो/ौ signs can need a small manual fix after pasting into Word (font set to Preeti). Common words and matras convert correctly.

Typing cheat-sheet (click to open)

Rules: double letters make long vowels (aa→ा, ii/ee→ी, uu/oo→ू) · capitals for retroflex (T ट, D ड, N ण) · ri = रि, Ri = ृ · common conjuncts join automatically (kram→क्रम, namaste→नमस्ते). One tap converts as you type.
