ℹ️ How this is worked out

  • Tax is charged slab by slab, not on the whole amount — so only the money above each threshold is taxed at the higher rate.
  • Individuals contributing to a retirement fund get a 1% rate on the first slab instead of the normal rate.
  • Married couples filing together usually get a higher first threshold.
  • SSF/EPF contribution is typically 11% from the employee and 20% from the employer.

The slab figures here follow the commonly published annual rates. Tax rules are set every year in the budget, so confirm the current year's numbers with the Inland Revenue Department before you rely on them for filing.
