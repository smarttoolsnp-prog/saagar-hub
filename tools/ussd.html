💡 Using USSD on a Nepali SIM

  • Type the code exactly, with the * at the start and # at the end, then press call.
  • The reply appears on screen as a message — no internet needed.
  • Balance checks and USSD requests are free. You only pay when you actually buy a pack.
  • A code that does nothing usually means your SIM is on a plan that hides it — call customer care.
  • For data packs the operator app shows more than USSD can, including price and validity.
🔒 Never give your security code (SCODE) or recharge PIN to anyone — not even someone claiming to be from the operator. They are only needed when you transfer balance or recharge a number.
