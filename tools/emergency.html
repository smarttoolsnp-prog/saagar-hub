Emergency Numbers

📞 National short codes

These short codes work from any Nepali mobile or landline without an area code.

ℹ️ What to say when you call

  1. Say where you are first — district, ward, and the nearest landmark or chowk. That is the most useful thing you can give them.
  2. Say what happened: accident, fire, medical emergency, crime in progress.
  3. Say how many people are hurt and whether anyone is trapped.
  4. Give a callback number and stay reachable.
  5. Do not hang up until they tell you to.
Numbers can change. These are the widely published national helplines, but operators and offices do update them. For anything official, cross-check with the Nepal Police, the Ministry of Health or your local ward office. In a real emergency, if one number does not answer, try the others — and ask a neighbour or local shop for the nearest police post.
