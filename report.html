Report a problem

Bugs · wrong numbers · ads · abuse · legal issues

Something broken, wrong or upsetting? Tell us. Reports about people (abuse, threats, scams in a room) are read before everything else.

Send reports to

dahalsaagar8@gmail.com · subject line: SAAGAR report

What to include (this makes it fixable in one round)

  • Page link — the exact URL, for example sagardahal123.com.np/games/ludo.html.
  • What happened — one or two plain sentences. "The dice never moved after I tapped roll."
  • What you expected — the correct behaviour.
  • Phone and browser — for example "Redmi 9A, Chrome 126, 4G" or "iPhone 12, Safari".
  • Screenshot or screen recording — optional but the single most useful thing you can add.
  • Room code — for anything inside a game room, plus roughly what time it happened (Nepal time if you can).
  • Ad problems — page link, what the ad did (covered content, wouldn't close, inappropriate content), and the ad's "Why this ad" link if there is one. Never click an ad just to test it.

Wrong information (rates, dates, laws)

If a figure or a rule is out of date, send the page link plus an official source — the ministry notice, the act, the bank's rate page, or a screenshot of it. Your report becomes the "last updated" fix on that page, with credit if you want it.

Abuse in a room, chat or voice

  • Tell us the room code, the display name used, the time, and what was said or done.
  • You do not need to send screenshots of upsetting content to a chat message that gets read — you can describe it in words.
  • Serious cases (threats, sexual content involving minors, scams, illegal content) are preserved and may be reported to the platform or to the authorities. That is why we ask for the time and the room code.
  • You can report anonymously. You can also ask us to keep you out of any follow-up.

Copyright or legal complaints

If you believe something on SAAGAR infringes your rights, email the same address with the subject line "Legal notice": the page link, the work you own, why you believe it is infringed, and your contact details. We aim to reply within 7 days and will remove or correct material where the complaint is valid.

What happens next

  1. You get an acknowledgement (usually within 2 working days, faster for abuse reports).
  2. We reproduce the problem on a real phone where possible.
  3. You get a short reply: fixed, planned, or "we could not reproduce it — here is what we need".
  4. Substantial fixes appear in the site changelog.

Privacy of your report

Reports are kept private. We do not publish who reported what. Personal details you send are used only to understand and fix the problem — see the privacy policy.

Also useful: the contact page for normal questions, the community rules for what is allowed in rooms, and the about page for how the site works.
