🧠 G·K Knowledge

General knowledge — Nepal first, then the world. Read the facts, then test yourself.

🇳🇵 Nepal — must-know facts

  • Nepal's flag is the only national flag in the world that is not rectangular — it's two stacked triangles.
  • Eight of the world's ten highest peaks are in Nepal, including Mount Everest (8,848.86 m) — called Sagarmatha in Nepali.
  • Nepal was never colonised by any country — a rare thing in Asia.
  • The capital Kathmandu was once a lake, according to legend drained by Manjushri cutting the Chobar gorge.
  • Nepal follows the Bikram Sambat calendar — about 56.7 years ahead of the Gregorian calendar. New Year falls in mid-April.
  • Lumbini in Nepal is the birthplace of Gautama Buddha.
  • Nepal's time zone is UTC+5:45 — one of only three places in the world using a :45 offset.
  • The living goddess Kumari is worshipped in Kathmandu — a real tradition found nowhere else.

🌍 World — quick facts

  • The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean — bigger than all land combined.
  • Venus is the hottest planet, not Mercury — its atmosphere traps heat.
  • The Great Wall of China is over 21,000 km long including all its branches.
  • Humans share about 60% of DNA with bananas.
  • The shortest war in history lasted 38 minutes (Britain vs Zanzibar, 1896).
