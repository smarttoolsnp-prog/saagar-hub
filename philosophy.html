🕯️ Photos · Philosophy

Short thoughts about life — paired with views worth pausing for.

🌊 On comparison

A river never compares itself to the sea — it just keeps flowing, and one day it arrives. Your timeline is not anyone else's timeline. Stop measuring your day 3 with someone's year 10.

⛰️ On struggle

The mountain does not apologise for being steep. The climb is the point — the version of you that reaches the top is built by every step that hurt. Struggle is not punishment; it's preparation.

🕐 On time

You will never be younger than you are at this exact second — and never more experienced than tomorrow. Both facts should scare you into starting, not freeze you into waiting.

🪞 On people

Be like salt — present in small amounts, but if you're missing, everyone notices. You don't need to be the loudest voice in the room to be the most remembered one.

🌱 On growth

A tree that is afraid of losing its leaves can never grow new ones. Let go of the habits, people and fears that stopped serving you. Empty branches are how new beginnings look at first.
