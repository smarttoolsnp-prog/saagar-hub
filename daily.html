🎯 Daily challenge

Five questions · one result card · a new set every midnight (Nepal time)

How the daily challenge works

  • One set a day. Everyone who opens this page on the same day gets the same five questions, so you can compare results with a friend.
  • Nepal time. The challenge resets at midnight Nepal Standard Time (UTC+5:45), not at whatever time zone you happen to be in.
  • Mixed questions. Three Nepal general-knowledge questions plus two logic puzzles — numbers, riddles and patterns that need no internet to solve.
  • No sign-up, nothing to install. Your streak, XP, level and badges are saved in this browser only. Clearing site data resets them.
  • Wordle-style share. The copied result shows coloured squares only — 🟩 and 🟥 — so the answers are never spoiled for the person you send it to.

Streaks, XP and badges

Each correct answer is 10 XP; a perfect day adds 25 more. Every 100 XP is a level. Play on consecutive days to build a streak — 3 days, 7 days and 30 days each unlock a badge, and a flawless day earns the 💎 badge. Rewards are cosmetic: there is no money, no purchase and nothing to cash out anywhere on SAAGAR.

Why a daily habit is worth it

A five-question habit takes under two minutes and quietly builds two useful things: general knowledge about Nepal (for school, for Loksewa-style exams, for everyday conversation) and the pattern-recognition that logic puzzles train. The emoji result makes it easy to share with a friend and compare — which is usually what makes a streak survive the first week.

Looking for something longer?

When the day's five questions are done, the skills guides show full roadmaps for real skills, the Nepal laws section explains everyday rights in plain language, and the games section has Ludo, Carrom, Snakes & Ladders, Checkers, Connect 4 and 2048 — solo, against a bot, or in a room with a friend.

Questions people ask

Can I play yesterday's challenge?

No — the daily set is deliberately only for today, which is what makes the streak meaningful. Every past day's questions come back around in later sets.

Do you store my score on a server?

No. The streak, XP and badges live in your browser's local storage. Nothing about your answers is uploaded — see the privacy policy.

How do I report a wrong answer?

Please do — use the report page with the question text. Facts get checked, and wrong questions are removed from the bank the same day.

What does the share card contain?

A 1080×1080 PNG with your score, emoji grid, streak and the site address — made in your browser, saved or shared from your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
