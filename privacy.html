Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · sagardahal123.com.np ("SAAGAR")

Your privacy matters. This page explains, in simple language, what information SAAGAR collects, what it does not collect, and how advertising cookies work. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect (very little)

SAAGAR is a free games and tools website that runs almost entirely inside your browser. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, address, or phone number.

  • Local storage: games and tools save small settings on your own device — for example your high score, best time, or preferred language. This data stays in your browser and never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Peer-to-peer features: some games let two friends connect directly (WebRTC). The connection is between the two browsers; our servers do not store your gameplay or chat.
  • Server logs: like almost every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type for security.

2. Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

We may show advertisements served by Google AdSense to keep this site free. When advertising is active:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick/DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • You can also block or delete cookies any time in your browser settings — the games and tools will still work.

Google's own advertising rules are explained here: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Children's privacy

Our games are family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Your choices

  • Clear cookies/local storage in your browser to reset everything this site saved on your device.
  • Opt out of personalised Google ads using the links in section 2.
  • Contact us any time with privacy questions (section 6).

5. Changes to this policy

If we add advertising partners or new features, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.

6. Contact
