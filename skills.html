Skills for Nepali Youth

Not a list of buzzwords — 15 skills that actually earn in Nepal, each with a full roadmap, realistic income, the mistakes everyone makes, and free links. Pick one. Give it 90 days. One hour a day.

पहिले यो पढ्नुहोस् — how to use this page

One skill at a time. The fastest way to stay poor in skills is to start five of them. Read all 15, choose one that fits your situation (your phone, your district, your family situation), and commit to 90 days. One hour a day, every day, beats 8 hours on Saturday.

Your phone is enough to start. Almost everything here — typing, English, Canva, CapCut, coding practice, Lok Sewa MCQs, MeroShare — works on an Android phone with 4 GB data a month. A laptop becomes necessary later, and by then your skill may already be paying for it.

Nepal tax, Nepal reward. Skills that everyone has (driving, basic MS Word) pay little. Skills that few can do well (real coding, real editing, real English, clean accounting, modern farming) pay a lot — and most of them pay in foreign currency if you are good. That is the whole game: be in the top 10% of one useful skill, not the average of ten.

Document everything. From day one, keep proof of what you make — screenshots, files, a portfolio folder. In Nepal nobody asks “what do you know?”; they ask “show me.”
